# First offline ML experiment — October 15, 2026

## Decision

Three small offline logistic models were fitted and evaluated once using the fixed `offline-ml-baseline-1` protocol. **No model was activated.** This experiment does not demonstrate a reliable predictive or trading edge. Tiny improvements in probability error for baseline and Fast30 coexist with near-chance ranking; early activity is worse than the constant probability benchmark. Selected quoted-return averages are sensitive to individual winners.

## Probability results at the 30-second quote horizon

Brier score and log loss evaluate probability estimates; smaller is better. AUC measures ranking; 0.5 is chance-level ranking. The constant comparator predicts the positive-label fraction in that cohort's training data. The training priors are all below 0.5, so its fixed-threshold selection is empty.

| Cohort | Training rows | Test watches | Valid test labels | Model Brier | Constant Brier | Model AUC |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Baseline | 118 | 77 | 77 | 0.2386 | 0.2396 | 0.5163 |
| Early activity | 121 | 92 | 87 | 0.2421 | 0.2338 | 0.4931 |
| Fast30 | 141 | 90 | 89 | 0.2260 | 0.2283 | 0.5026 |

Daily results vary materially. These small differences are not a significance claim. The target is a positive **quoted** return conditional on an on-time validated quote, not successful real execution or net profit.

## Fixed 0.5 probability threshold

| Cohort | Selected test watches | Valid selected quotes | Mean quoted return | Median | Mean excluding best quote |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Baseline | 20 | 20 | +0.70% | −0.78% | −0.49% |
| Early activity | 31 | 31 | −5.10% | −4.68% | −6.53% |
| Fast30 | 8 | 7 | +15.78% | +1.01% | −0.01% |

Fast30's eighth selected case had a failed sell quote. It remains in the selected denominator and is not counted as zero, a win or a loss. Its apparent positive mean among the seven returned quotes disappears when the single best result is removed. Baseline's small positive mean is also sensitive to its best result. No threshold was tuned after seeing these results.

Existing verified qualification labels cover only 20 baseline and 24 early-activity test watches, and no Fast30 watches. Their retain/defer breakdown is retained in the full report; it is not a comparable full-population probability benchmark. The baseline retained subset has only two watches and cannot establish rule superiority.

## Development direction

Keep this benchmark as a reproducible reference. Do not promote these models or search the same holdout for a winning threshold. A next model iteration should address entry-feature coverage and provenance, particularly unavailable liquidity and security context in the early cohorts, while preserving missing values explicitly. Any candidate eventually proposed for forward evaluation needs frozen features, preprocessing, weights, policy and a new untouched evaluation interval. Quote availability and execution remain separate questions.
